Fine tuning

Stamp size scales everything together — box, text and map. It stops growing once the panel reaches the edges of the photo, which on a normal portrait shot is around 100%, because the original stamp already spans the full width. To make the lettering bigger in place, use Text size; the box grows taller to suit. Box width / height reshape just the panel. Everything at 100% is the exact original.

On the preview: drag a corner to scale the whole stamp, the right edge for width, the top edge for height.

All sizes are proportional to the photo's width, so the stamp lands identically on a 1200px or a 4000px image.

Inter didn’t load. The stamp is falling back to a system font, so the lettering won’t match the original exactly. Connect to the internet and reload, or keep going if an approximate match is fine.
